# Early Bird Program Terms

Version 1.0 · effective 16 October 2026

The Early Bird Program is for the first 10 Organizations that use agere/org. These terms apply together with the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy. If they conflict, these terms apply to participants (Terms of Service Article 1.5).

## Summary

- For 10 Organizations of 3 to 15 people. Registration closes on 8 January 2027.
- Every Team plan feature free for 90 days, with no payment method required.
- AI Credits match the Team plan: 100 per Active member a month, with a minimum of 300 and a maximum of 1,500.
- If you subscribe to the Team plan no later than 7 days after the early bird period ends, your price per Seat will not go up for 12 months: Rp59,000 per Seat per month billed yearly, or Rp69,000 per Seat per month billed monthly.
- If you don't subscribe, your Organization moves to the Free plan. No data is deleted.

This summary is for convenience. The articles below are binding.

## 1. Who can join

1. Organizations or businesses in Indonesia with 3 to 15 people who will use agere/org for their daily work.
2. There are 10 places. We choose participants after a 30-minute introductory call with an agere founder, and send the result to the applicant by email.
3. Registration closes on 8 January 2027, or earlier if all places are filled.
4. A business can join only once. Participant status cannot be moved to another Organization.

## 2. Early bird period

1. We activate participants between 19 January and 2 February 2027 and email the activation date to the Owner.
2. The early bird period lasts 90 days. The activation date counts as day one. For example, if you are activated on 19 January 2027, the last day of your early bird period is 18 April 2027.
3. By mutual agreement, a participant can start using agere/org from 11 December 2026. In that case, the 90 days and the AI Credit allowance still start on 19 January 2027. Before that date, features that are already available can be used at no cost, and some features, including AI features, may not be available yet.
4. The early bird period replaces the 14-day trial in Terms of Service Article 5. The two are not combined.

## 3. What you get during the early bird period

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| Every Team plan feature | Free, with no payment method and no invoice |
| AI Credits | 100 credits per Active member a month, with a minimum of 300 and a maximum of 1,500, granted on the 1st of each month based on the number of Active members that day. In the first month, the allowance is proportional to the days left in that month. Unused credits don't carry over. Extra credits are not available when the program starts. If they become available, participants can buy them under Terms of Service Article 7.6; purchased credits don't end with the early bird period |
| Support | Through support@agereorg.com, answered within 2 business days, the same as the Team plan |
| New features | First access to features marked "Coming soon" when they are ready. This is not a promise of a release date |
| Access to the product team | We discuss your feedback in feedback sessions and consider it when we plan development. This is not a promise that any specific feature will be built |

Other AI Credit rules follow Terms of Service Article 7. When AI Credits run out, only AI features stop until the next allowance. Everything else keeps working.

If you have more than 15 Active members during the early bird period, the benefits still apply until the period ends. We will contact the Owner to discuss what comes next.

## 4. Continuing on the Team plan

1. The Owner or an Admin can pay for the Team plan at any time during the early bird period, or no later than 7 days after it ends.
2. The first paid period starts on the day the payment succeeds. Any remaining free days are not added to the paid period.
3. Once you subscribe, your Organization follows the Team plan under the Terms of Service. Your AI Credit allowance stays at 100 per Seat a month; the 1,500 maximum applies only during the early bird period.
4. Seats are counted from Active members, with a minimum of 3 (Terms of Service Article 6.2).

## 5. Price locked for 12 months

1. If you pay within the deadline in Article 4.1, your price per Seat is locked for every Billing period that starts within 12 months of the start of your first paid period:
    - Rp59,000 per Seat per month on the yearly cycle (Rp708,000 per Seat per year);
    - Rp69,000 per Seat per month on the monthly cycle.
2. This means that if the Team plan price goes up during those 12 months, you keep paying the prices above. The locked price is not a discount on the current price.
3. The locked price applies to every Seat in the Organization, including Seats you add, and to both cycles. Changing cycles follows Terms of Service Article 6.5.
4. The locked price ends early if the subscription is canceled and its period runs out, or if the Organization moves to the Free plan because an invoice was not paid after the grace period.
5. The locked price cannot be combined with other discounts or promotions, and cannot be moved to another Organization.
6. The prices above are the amounts you pay per Seat.

## 6. If the early bird period ends without payment

1. We remind the Owner and Admins by email and in the app 14 days and 3 days before the early bird period ends.
2. If no payment has been made when the early bird period ends, the Organization moves to the Free plan under Terms of Service Article 6.6. Organizations with 6 or more Active members get 7 days of grace before members are paused, the same as after a trial.
3. No data is deleted. Everything that was paused or made read-only comes back when the Organization subscribes to the Team plan.
4. Any unused early bird AI Credit allowance ends with the early bird period. Credits you purchased do not.

## 7. What we ask of participants

1. Use agere/org for your team's real work, not only to try it out.
2. Join a 30-minute feedback session every 2 weeks and fill in short surveys. This is a request, not a requirement, and it does not affect your benefits.
3. Report problems to support@agereorg.com.
4. Don't share material we mark as confidential, such as designs for features that have not been released.

## 8. Other things to know

1. The Service is new and may change. Like the Team plan, the early bird period has no service level agreement (SLA) (Terms of Service Article 12).
2. We won't name your Organization or use its logo in public materials without written permission.
3. We may end early bird benefits if Terms of Service Article 11 is breached, after notifying the Owner.
4. Changes to these terms follow Terms of Service Article 17. Changes that are worse for you don't apply to participants who have already been activated unless they agree.
5. These terms are written in Indonesian and English. If the two differ in meaning, the Indonesian version prevails.
6. Questions about the program: support@agereorg.com.
