# FAQ

## What is agere/org?

A workspace for small teams: spaces, projects, and tasks on a board or list, Desk for your tasks and inbox, and AI that breaks a goal into suggested tasks you approve before they are created.

## What is the early bird program?

A program for the first 10 Organizations of 3 to 15 people. Participants use every Team plan feature free for 90 days and help shape the product through feedback sessions.

## How do I join?

Apply with the form on this page. We invite suitable applicants to a 30-minute introductory call and send the result by email. Registration closes on 8 January 2027, or earlier if all places are filled.

## When can I start using agere/org?

Participants are activated between 19 January and 2 February 2027. The 90 days start on the activation date.

## Do I need a card or payment method?

No. The early bird period requires no payment method and issues no invoices.

## What are AI Credits?

The unit of usage for AI features. Early bird participants get 100 credits per Active member a month, with a minimum of 300 and a maximum of 1,500, granted on the 1st of each month. Unused credits don't carry over to the next month.

## What happens if AI Credits run out?

Only AI features stop until the next allowance. Spaces, projects, tasks, and every other feature keep working.

## What happens after 90 days?

You choose. Continue on the Team plan, and your price per Seat is locked for 12 months if you pay no later than 7 days after the early bird period ends. Or don't continue, and your Organization moves to the Free plan. No data is deleted.

## Is our data used to train AI?

No. We use only model providers that do not retain the data sent to them, and email addresses and account identity are never sent to the model. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

## Which payment methods are available if we continue?

QRIS, virtual accounts, and cards through our payment partner. We do not store your card details.

## How do I contact you?

Email support@agereorg.com. We reply within 2 business days.
